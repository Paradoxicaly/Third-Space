# Development Notes

## Core experience

1. Browse nearby third spaces.
2. Filter by atmosphere, amenities, and accessibility.
3. Open a place page to read community reviews and practical details.
4. Save places to revisit later.

## Initial priorities

- Build a simple, welcoming discovery experience.
- Keep listings useful with accurate hours, amenities, and location details.
- Make reviews constructive and easy for the community to trust.
